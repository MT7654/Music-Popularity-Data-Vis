Global Vibe Recommendations

1️⃣ Momentum vs Popularity

Analysis: Around 40% of the tracks cluster in the mid-to-high popularity range, but only a handful push above the 70-mark in both momentum and popularity. EDM and high-energy pop stand out - they’re not just popular but also accelerating fast, signaling repeat listens and algorithmic boosts. Lower-momentum vibes like acoustic or lo-fi remain steady but aren’t showing much viral movement right now.

2️⃣ Popularity Trends Over Time

Analysis: Over the last 6-8 months, dance and pop vibes have seen a consistent upward climb, averaging a 20-25% increase in popularity. Acoustic and mellow styles fluctuate more, rising briefly during quieter months before tapering off. The overall trend shows a clear audience shift toward energetic, replayable tracks that perform better on short-form platforms.

3️⃣ Latest Momentum by Country

Analysis: Momentum trends vary sharply across regions. In Australia, EDM tracks score around 75 on average, while Canada and the U.S. hover near 70. In contrast, European countries lean toward softer styles - acoustic and chill vibes hold steady between 50 and 60 momentum. This split suggests two emerging listener patterns: upbeat streaming habits in Western markets and introspective, slower preferences in parts of Europe and Asia.
