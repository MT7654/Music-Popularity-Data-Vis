Understanding the Scatterplot

Each point on the scatterplot represents an artist. The horizontal axis shows the artist’s momentum (higher = faster growth). The vertical axis reflects popularity — lower = better.

Bottom-right = popular & rising, top-right = rising but not yet mainstream, top-left = low growth & low exposure, bottom-left = stable & established.
