Music Vibes Around the World

Explore the vibe distribution across France, Japan, Brazil, Indonesia, the U.S., and India.

Insights

  • Dance / EDM emerges as a global favorite, dominating in India (0.70), Brazil (0.55), and the U.S. (0.53). These regions share a strong affinity for energetic and festival-style music.
  • Japan stands apart with its overwhelming love for High-Energy Pop (0.65), nearly triple that of France (0.24) — reflecting Japan’s deep-rooted pop culture and polished mainstream sound.
  • Indonesia contrasts sharply with Japan, leading in Emo / Sad Chill (0.82) while Japan ranks the lowest (0.04). This shows a clear divide between emotional introspection and upbeat optimism in listener preferences.
  • France maintains the highest share of Alternative / Experimental (0.50) music, signaling a taste for creative, non-mainstream styles — opposite of Brazil’s focus on rhythm-heavy Dance / EDM (0.55).
  • Classic / Evergreen music thrives in Indonesia (0.78) and the U.S. (0.43), showing nostalgia and appreciation for timeless tracks even as newer genres grow.
  • Overall, Rap / Trap Vibes remain underrepresented across all regions — highest in France (0.33) yet minimal elsewhere — suggesting that lyrical, beat-driven genres are still niche compared to melodic or electronic styles.
